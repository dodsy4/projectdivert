Method, and what this is not

The model is attributional and cradle-to-grave. The baseline is what diversion avoids: landfill disposal plus the haul to the landfill the material would otherwise have used. The diversion scenario is what it costs: the haul to the reprocessor or reuse destination, the reprocessing or reuse-preparation energy, and a negative term crediting the virgin production the recovered material displaces. Net avoided emissions are the baseline minus the diversion scenario, so a positive number is a benefit.

Every factor is secondary data from a published dataset — UK DESNZ conversion factors, the ICE embodied-carbon database, and WRAP — and each is cited above with its year and data quality. Transport is modelled one-way and laden, with no allocation of the return leg. Capital goods, commuting, packaging and the use phase are outside the boundary.

This is an attributional LCA built on published secondary data. It has not had the independent critical review that ISO 14044 §6.3 requires for a comparative assertion disclosed to the public. Treat the results as internal decision support, not as a certified LCA.
